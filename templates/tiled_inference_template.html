📌 Spatial Clustering Insights

High-resolution real images often present concentrated clusters of small targets. The spatial dispersion metric evaluates normalized pairwise distances between all object centroids in each scene:

  • Clustered (Muy Agrupado, < 0.20): Objects are concentrated in localized mechanical assemblies or nests.
  • Moderate (Moderado, 0.20 - 0.40): Standard spread with balanced distances between objects.
  • Dispersed (Disperso, > 0.40): Objects widely distributed across the frame margins.
